FUTURE READY • BLACK HILLS STEAM SHOWCASE

The Future of the Black Hills Is Already Here.

Every day, students walk through the doors of Douglas Middle School with questions about the world around them.

How are bridges built?

Who designs airplanes?

What does it take to become a nurse, an engineer, a welder, a wildlife biologist, or a pilot?

Those questions deserve real conversations with the people who live those careers every day.

By connecting students with local businesses, industries, colleges, military organizations, healthcare providers, public agencies, and community leaders, we hope to inspire students to see the possibilities waiting for them right here in the Black Hills.

One conversation.

One demonstration.

One experience.

Sometimes that's all it takes to change the direction of a student's future.

You Are Part of Our Students' Future.

The Black Hills is filled with remarkable people solving real problems every day.

You build.

You heal.

You discover.

You protect.

You teach.

You create.

You innovate.

Whether your organization mines minerals, researches the universe, cares for patients, builds homes, protects natural resources, develops technology, serves our country, or educates the next generation, your work helps shape the future of our community.

We believe students should meet the people behind those careers.

Not because we expect every student to choose the same path...

But because every student deserves to know what is possible.

That is why we are inviting a select group of organizations to become Founding Community Partners in the Future Ready: Black Hills STEAM Showcase.

Our Invitation

Whether your organization serves hundreds of people or a handful each day, your experience has the power to inspire students to see new possibilities for their future.

Future Ready is about building connections between classrooms and the incredible organizations that make the Black Hills such a remarkable place to live, work, and innovate.

Every student deserves to know what is possible.
